# CDMO Capability Database

A verified reference on three major Indian CDMO players, built for a sourcing and
procurement company. It answers, per competitor: what they can make, the chemistries
and technology platforms they run, the products and intermediates they supply, and the
hard metrics that qualify them.

Everything is categorised **broad to narrow**:

`Company > Business Segment > Capability Domain > Chemistry or Capability > Product or Intermediate`

- Machine readable database: [`data/cdmo_capabilities.json`](../data/cdmo_capabilities.json)
- In app view: **CDMO Capabilities** page in the sidebar (`/cdmo-capabilities`)
- Last updated: 2026-07-02

> Figures carry the fiscal year and an as-of date where the source dates them. Verify
> DMF counts, certifications, and capacity against the latest filings before contractual use.

## 1. Divi's Laboratories Limited

One of the world's largest merchant API and custom synthesis manufacturers. Deeply
backward integrated, cost led, focused on high barrier chemistry. Not a discovery CRDMO.

- HQ: Hyderabad, Telangana. Founded 1990 (first plant 1995). Listed NSE/BSE (DIVISLAB).
- Revenue FY25: ~9,712 cr total income (~1.15 billion USD); PAT ~2,191 cr.
- 17,000+ employees, 700+ scientists, 400+ PhDs, 4 R&D centers, serves 95+ countries.
- Combined reactor volume 18,600+ m3.
- Regulatory (as of 31 Mar 2024): 41 US-FDA DMFs, 25 Health Canada DMFs, 26 EDQM CoS, 7 PMDA DMFs. cGMP, ISO 9001, ISO 14001.

**Segments:** Generic APIs; Custom Synthesis (CDMO); Nutraceuticals (carotenoids).

**Sites:** Unit I Choutuppal, Telangana (1995); Unit II Chippada near Visakhapatnam, AP,
with export SEZ units (2002); Unit III near Kakinada, AP (Jan 2025).

**Technical capabilities and chemistries**

- Technology platforms: continuous flow (scaling to commercial), biocatalysis, photochemistry, high pressure, solid phase peptide synthesis (multiple 500 L reactors).
- Chemistry toolbox: halogenation, hydrogenation, chiral and asymmetric synthesis, organometallic (Grignard), ozonolysis, peptide coupling, iodination, multi step complex synthesis.
- Containment: OEB-4 and OEB-5 HPAPI suites.
- Particle engineering: milling and micronization, particle size distribution, polymorph control.
- Modalities: small molecules, peptides (GLP-1 focus), contrast media.

**Products and intermediates**

- Generic APIs: Naproxen and Dextromethorphan (each estimated 65 to 85 percent global share), Gabapentin, Levetiracetam, Valsartan, Pregabalin, Orlistat.
- Intermediates: GLP-1 peptide intermediates, iodinated contrast media intermediates.
- Nutraceuticals: full carotenoid range (commonly beta carotene, lycopene, lutein, astaxanthin, canthaxanthin, apo ester).

**Sourcing angle:** best for large volume high barrier generic APIs and complex custom
synthesis where scale, cost, and reliability matter. Note single source concentration in
naproxen and dextromethorphan. Rising option for GLP-1 and contrast media intermediates.

## 2. Sai Life Sciences Limited

Integrated CRDMO for small molecule new chemical entities, discovery to commercial, on a
hub and spoke model (India scale plus UK and US front end research).

- HQ: Hyderabad, Telangana. Founded 1999. IPO December 2024 (SAILIFE).
- Revenue FY25: ~1,695 cr (~200 million USD), up ~16 percent; PAT up ~105 percent.
- 3,400+ scientists and professionals. Pipeline 170+ molecules, 38 late stage, 7 commercial.
- Bidar reactor capacity ~700 KL (after Block 11 Phase II, Q1 FY26). Bidar inspected by US FDA and PMDA.

**Segments:** Discovery Services (MedChem, Biology, DMPK, Toxicology); Development and CMC
(process development, scale up, early phase supplies); Manufacturing (cGMP intermediates and APIs).

**Sites:** Bidar, Karnataka (flagship manufacturing); integrated R&D campus, Hyderabad;
Manchester R&D center, UK (2020); Watertown discovery site, Greater Boston, USA (2020).

**Technical capabilities and chemistries**

- Technology platforms: flow chemistry, biocatalysis (invested niche areas).
- Chemistry toolbox: complex multi step small molecule synthesis, chiral synthesis, hazardous reactions via flow.
- Containment: HPAPI capability (confirm OEB banding).
- Modalities: small molecule NCEs (core).
- Service scope: discovery, process development and CMC, clinical to commercial manufacturing.

**Products and intermediates:** confidential innovator NCE programs rather than a public
generic catalog. Output is client owned advanced intermediates and clinical to commercial APIs.

**Sourcing angle:** best when you need one integrated partner to move a novel small molecule
from discovery to commercial with a single tech transfer, valuing UK and US research
proximity. Not an off the shelf generic API source.

## 3. Cohance Lifesciences Limited

Integrated CRDMO and API platform with the widest modality range of the three: small
molecules, HPAPI, ADCs, oligonucleotides, and specialty chemicals. Advent International backed.

- HQ: Hyderabad, Telangana. Built through acquisitions (ZCL Chemicals, Avra Laboratories, Suven Pharmaceuticals, Sapala Organics, NJ Bio); merged with Suven in 2025 to form Cohance Lifesciences (NSE/BSE: COHANCE).
- Revenue: ~2,392 cr combined in FY24; management target ~6,000 cr by FY29; roughly one billion dollar scale CDMO.
- 20+ facilities across India and the US; 8 manufacturing units (5 US FDA approved), 2 R&D units; 200+ DMFs filed globally.
- ADC capacity: cGMP bioconjugation suite up to ~2 kg ADC (to Phase 2b).

**Segments:** Pharma CDMO (small molecule); Specialty Chemicals CDMO; APIs; ADCs and
Oligonucleotides; Formulations.

**Sites:** API and CDMO units at Jaggayyapeta and Atchutapuram, AP, plus Hyderabad area;
Genome Valley R&D, Hyderabad (400 MHz NMR, HPLC, flow reactors); Sapala Organics, Hyderabad
(oligonucleotides); NJ Bio, Princeton, US (ADC discovery to CRDMO).

**Technical capabilities and chemistries**

- Technology platforms: flow chemistry, high pressure, cryogenics, bioconjugation.
- Chemistry toolbox: chiral synthesis, complex multi step synthesis, nucleic acid / oligonucleotide chemistry, Camptothecin (CPT) derivative chemistry with proprietary S-Trione technology.
- Containment: high containment suites for potent cytotoxic payloads and conjugates.
- Modalities: small molecules, HPAPI, ADCs (payloads, linkers, conjugation via NJ Bio), oligonucleotides.

**Products and intermediates**

- Broad API and intermediate catalog (200+ DMFs) plus specialty and fine chemicals.
- Platform products: Camptothecin (CPT) derived ADC payloads, S-Trione linker payload technology, oligonucleotide building blocks.

**Sourcing angle:** the go to option for ADC payloads and conjugation, oligonucleotides,
HPAPI, and specialty chemicals CDMO alongside conventional small molecule APIs. Newly
merged, so confirm which unit or legacy business holds a given capability or filing.
